Satellite Constellation Solar Power Modeling Under Earth Occultation

Objectives

Model how Earth shadowing affects solar power generation for a low-orbit satellite constellation.

Outcomes & Contributions

Simulated a 3-plane, 9-satellite constellation over 10 days and found that satellites avoiding Earth’s shadow received about 13% more power on average.

Technical Details & Skills

MATLAB, orbital mechanics, vector geometry, solar irradiance modeling, umbra and penumbra modeling, tensor-based variables.

Modeling Uni-Cellular Organism Movement Using a Hidden Markov Chain

Objectives

Develop a stochastic process framework to predict long-term, multi-directional organism movement behavior.

Outcomes & Contributions

Built a scalable Hidden Markov Model with boundary conditions and generalized transition matrices for arbitrary state spaces and angular variations.

Technical Details & Skills

MATLAB, Hidden Markov Models, stochastic processes, Markov chains, spectral decomposition, eigenvalue analysis, steady-state behavior.
